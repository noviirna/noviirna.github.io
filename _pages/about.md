        <p>I am an IT practitioner with experience spanning development, systems analysis, technical leadership, and solution architecture. From there I went into a management trainee program at one of Indonesia's privately owned banks, then spent six years in software engineering.</p>

The tech lead role had the blurriest edges of the three, nobody drew clear lines around what belonged to that title, so I filled the gap myself: reviewing vendor contracts before a procurement decision one week, debugging a pipeline nobody else wanted to touch the next.</p>
        <p>That's the pattern more than any title. New stack, new process, new kind of problem, I get productive fast and don't get attached to one language or workflow. Engineering is the base, but the work keeps pulling me into architecture, planning, and writing things down for other people. Underneath all of it is the same instinct from that first infosec job at the bank.</p>
